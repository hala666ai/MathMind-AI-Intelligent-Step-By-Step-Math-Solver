## MathMind AI — Intelligent Step‑By‑Step Math Reasoning Engine

MathMind AI is an advanced natural‑language math reasoning engine that interprets written problems, performs symbolic analysis, and generates clear, human‑style explanations for every step. It solves algebra, calculus, and probability tasks, detects student errors, and adapts its teaching style for beginners or advanced learners. Designed as a lightweight, extensible AI tutor, MathMind AI bridges the gap between raw computation and real mathematical understanding.

---

## 🚀 Features

- 🧠 **NLP‑Powered Understanding**  
  Interprets math problems written in natural language with high accuracy.

- 📘 **Step‑By‑Step Explanations**  
  Produces human‑readable reasoning, not just final answers.

- 🧮 **Symbolic Math Engine**  
  Supports algebra, equation solving, simplification, derivatives, and basic probability.

- 🎓 **Adaptive Teaching Modes**  
  - **Kids Mode:** friendly, simplified explanations  
  - **Pro Mode:** technical, university‑level reasoning  

- ❗ **Student Error Detection**  
  Compares the student’s answer with the symbolic solution and highlights mistakes.

- 🔧 **Extensible Architecture**  
  Can be expanded into a chatbot, API, or integrated into educational platforms.
